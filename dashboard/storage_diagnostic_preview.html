Category Growth Diagnostic · Storage & Organisers · Noon AE

The category is growing on traffic —
and quietly losing it on conversion.

GMV is up +47.2% YoY (Jul'26 vs Jul'25, clean full-month comparison) on a genuinely broad base — traffic, conversion and pricing are all up year-on-year. But the most recent month breaks that pattern: in the last 30 days, three of the category's biggest subcategories — Storage Box, Racks, Storage Basket — suffered a sudden, sharp conversion crash with no precedent anywhere in the prior 19 months of data, and it's still live as of the latest data.

How to fix each stage — three separate levers, three separate teams
↑ Increase GV (traffic)

Fix CTR, not impressions — impression volume is already healthy in every problem subcat. Rewrite titles/thumbnails for noon_east and milton first (highest absolute impression waste); run a search-relevance/keyword audit on the 7 CTR-problem subcats. Not a paid-ads fix. ≈ AED 437K/mo (§12).

↑ Increase ATC

ATC-rate loss is 96% concentrated in the Big 3 (Racks, Storage Box, Storage Basket) — this is the same August event, not a separate PDP problem elsewhere. Audit primary image, price badge and size/spec clarity on the highest-GV SKUs in these three subcats first.

↑ Increase CVR

Start with the dish-drying-rack duplicate-listing cluster inside Racks (§06) — 6 near-identical SKUs converting 0–2% on real traffic. Then verify no price/stock sync fault broke checkout for Storage Box and Storage Basket specifically in the last cycle.

14

Category Growth Plan

A · Diagnosis — what is happening

Storage & Organisers is in genuine, broad-based growth — GMV is up 47.2% YoY (Jul'26 vs Jul'25, clean) and 17.5% MoM, with traffic, conversion and pricing all contributing positively YoY. That healthy picture broke in the most recent month: three subcategories that together carry 32% of category GMV — Storage Box, Racks, Storage Basket — each had a sudden, sharp conversion crash with no precedent in the prior 19 months of stable data, and add-to-cart rate fell 3.3pp MoM even as visits surged.

B · Root causes — why

Three distinct, independently-evidenced mechanisms: (1) a genuine conversion-quality problem concentrated in a handful of high-visit subcategories and SKU clusters (e.g. duplicate dish-rack listings) — not price-driven, since discount depth shows no CVR benefit anywhere in the category; (2) a discoverability/CTR gap in seven mid-size subcategories that sit on healthy impression volume but convert those impressions to visits poorly, pointing at title/thumbnail/search-relevance rather than demand; (3) operational leakage — chronic OOS concentrated in a short list of SKUs and specifically in Express fulfilment (88.4% vs 95.6% instock), plus one clear brand-visibility incident (Cosmoplast) that looks like a listing/ranking fault, not competitive loss.
